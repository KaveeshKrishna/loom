# Loom for Android: changes

## 1.0.0

The first version of Loom for Android, for phones and tablets.

- **Loom in an app**: every part of Loom, made for touch, with uploads and downloads handed to the app's own transfer manager.
- **Transfers that finish**: uploads and downloads keep going with the app closed and the screen off, resume after a restart or a lost connection, and never upload a file twice. Pause, resume, cancel and retry them in Transfers or from the notification.
- **Straight over your home network** when this device and Loom are on the same network, and back over the internet when you leave.
- **Share › Loom** from any app, and an **Upload** shortcut on the app icon (touch and hold).
- **Whole folders** keep their structure. Files whose names are already taken ask before anything is replaced.
- **Tablets**: Transfers beside Loom on wide screens, and drag files from another app onto Loom in split screen.
- **Wi-Fi only** for big transfers, and a speed limit.
- **Sign in** by approving this device in Loom, or by scanning the pairing code from Loom's Devices page.
- **Updates itself** from GitHub: download and ask (the default), install automatically, or only tell you.
